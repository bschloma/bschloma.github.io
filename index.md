I am a James S. McDonnell postdoctoral fellow [Hernan Garcia's lab](http://garcialab.berkeley.edu) at UC Berkeley. I am a biophysicist studying gene expression dynamics during immune responses. I use live imaging to peer inside animals and watch immune responses at the single-cell level.
